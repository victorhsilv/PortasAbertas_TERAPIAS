Welcome to the Alternative Therapies Portal

Developed for ETEC de Hortolândia's “Portas Abertas” project, this website introduces complementary practices related to well-being, communication and integrated care.

The project explains three topics in an organized and accessible way: Music Therapy, Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP) and Dowsing. Each one has its own proposals, methods and contexts of use.

Music Therapy
NLP
Dowsing

Music Therapy

Music therapy uses music, rhythm, melody and sound in a structured professional process that can support communication, emotional expression, rehabilitation and quality of life.

NLP

Neuro-Linguistic Programming studies relationships among language, perception and learned behavioral patterns, and is commonly discussed in communication and personal-development settings.

Dowsing

Dowsing is a traditional practice that uses instruments such as pendulums or rods to interpret responses associated with objects, environments or claimed energy fields.
