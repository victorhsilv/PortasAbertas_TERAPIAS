Therapies and Applications

This page summarizes how the main alternative and integrative approaches appear in different contexts. The information is educational and serves as an introduction to the basic concepts of each practice.

1. Music Therapy

Music therapy uses music, sounds, rhythm and other musical experiences in a planned process led by a qualified professional.

  • Where it may appear: hospitals, clinics, schools, rehabilitation centers and social projects.
  • Possible goals: supporting emotional expression, communication and quality of life.

2. NLP (Neuro-Linguistic Programming)

NLP brings together communication models and techniques related to how people describe experiences, build meaning and organize behavior.

  • Where it may appear: communication training, personal development, leadership and sales.
  • Note: its claims should be evaluated critically and responsibly.

3. Dowsing

Dowsing is a traditional practice in which tools such as pendulums and rods are used to interpret responses associated with objects, environments or claimed energetic fields.

  • Where it commonly appears: holistic practices, alternative consultations and environmental assessments.
  • Note: it should not replace conventional health examinations or diagnoses.

Important: for health concerns, symptoms or emotional distress, seek appropriate professional guidance. Complementary practices should be treated as complements, not automatic substitutes for conventional medical care.
