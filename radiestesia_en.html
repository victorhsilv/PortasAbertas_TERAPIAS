Dowsing

Dowsing is a traditional practice in which instruments such as pendulums or rods are used to interpret movements associated with objects, environments or claimed energetic fields.

Origin: Historical Context

Rod-based searching practices have existed in different cultures for centuries. The modern term associated with radiesthesia became popular in France in the early 20th century.

French practitioners such as Alexis Bouly helped popularize the term and systematic use of pendulums and related charts.

Who Can Practice Dowsing?

In Brazil it is generally presented within holistic-practice settings rather than as a regulated medical profession.

  • People trained through independent courses in dowsing and related holistic practices.
  • Complementary-practice professionals who use it within non-medical contexts.

Uses and Care

Dowsing is commonly associated with environmental or holistic interpretations. Its energetic claims should not be treated as established medical evidence.

Common contexts:

  • Environmental or geobiology-style assessments.
  • Holistic well-being practices.
  • Complementary, non-diagnostic consultations.

Important limitations:

  • It must not be used to diagnose diseases or replace medical examinations.
  • Urgent symptoms require immediate professional medical care.
  • It should not be used compulsively to make every personal decision.
