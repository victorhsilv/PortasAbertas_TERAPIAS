About the Topic and the Project

What are alternative and complementary practices?

Integrative and Complementary Health Practices include different approaches used alongside conventional health care. They may focus on prevention, well-being and rehabilitation, while considering physical, emotional, mental, social and cultural dimensions.

This field is heterogeneous: practices differ greatly in scientific evidence, clinical validation and professional recognition.

For that reason, complementary practices should be distinguished from proven medical treatment. Persistent symptoms, intense distress or health conditions should be assessed by qualified professionals.

The three topics studied

  • NLP: explores relationships between language, perception and behavior, especially in communication and personal development.
  • Music Therapy: uses structured musical experiences, normally led by a qualified professional, to support therapeutic goals.
  • Dowsing: a traditional practice involving tools such as pendulums; its energetic claims do not have the same level of scientific support as established clinical therapies.

Project goal

The project presents concepts, possible applications, limits and research sources while encouraging visitors to compare perspectives without confusing educational information with medical advice.
