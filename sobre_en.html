About the Topic and the Project

What are alternative and complementary practices?

Integrative and Complementary Health Practices include different approaches used alongside conventional health care. They may focus on prevention, well-being and rehabilitation, while considering physical, emotional, mental, social and cultural dimensions.

This field is heterogeneous: practices differ greatly in scientific evidence, clinical validation and professional recognition.

For that reason, complementary practices should be distinguished from proven medical treatment. Persistent symptoms, intense distress or health conditions should be assessed by qualified professionals.

The three topics studied

  • NLP: explores relationships between language, perception and behavior, especially in communication and personal development.
  • Music Therapy: uses structured musical experiences, normally led by a qualified professional, to support therapeutic goals.
  • Dowsing: a traditional practice involving tools such as pendulums; its energetic claims do not have the same level of scientific support as established clinical therapies.

Project goal

The project presents concepts, possible applications, limits and research sources while encouraging visitors to compare perspectives without confusing educational information with medical advice.

Music Therapy

Music therapy and sound expression represent one of the most dynamic frontiers of integrative therapies, using music, rhythm, melody, and sound to rehabilitate, nurture, and transform mental and physical health. More than just an entertainment resource, the professional use of these tools is based on neuroscientific evidence: sound stimuli activate brain areas linked to emotions, memory, motor coordination, and language simultaneously.

NLP

Neuro-Linguistic Programming (NLP) represents one of the most widespread methodologies for human development, uniting the understanding of how the brain processes information (neuro), how language shapes our reality (linguistics), and how we organize our actions to achieve results (programming). Created in the 1970s by Richard Bandler and John Grinder, NLP studies the structure of subjective experience to decode and replicate human excellence.

Radiesthesia

Radiesthesia is an integrative and complementary practice focused on the perception, measurement, and interpretation of supposed energy fields and subtle vibrations present in living beings, objects, and environments. Based on the premise that everything that exists emits a magnetic or vibrational signature, it acts as a tool for bioenergetic diagnosis and harmonization. The term derives from the union of the Latin radius (ray/radiation) with the Greek aisthesis (perception or sensitivity).
